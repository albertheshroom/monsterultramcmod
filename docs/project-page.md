# Monster Ultra

Crack open a cold one in Minecraft! **Monster Ultra** adds Monster Ultra energy drinks – 16 flavors, each as a can and as a bucket.

## Flavors
Ultra White · Ultra Strawberry Dreams · Ultra Rosá · Ultra Paradise · Ultra Violet · Ultra Peachy Keen · Ultra Watermelon · Ultra Red · Ultra Blue · Ultra Fantasy Ruby Red · Lando Norris · Ultra Vice Guava · Ultra Fiesta Mango · VR46 Zero Sugar · Ultra Gold Pineapple · Ultra Black

## Features
- **Cans** – drink one for a little food plus Speed and Haste for 30 seconds. You keep the empty can.
- **Buckets** – a whole bucket of your favorite flavor.
- **Canning in the brewing stand** – put a Monster bucket in the top slot and iron nuggets in the three bottom slots. After 20 seconds the nuggets turn into cans of that flavor and you get the empty bucket back. No blaze powder needed.
- Own creative tab, English and Czech translations.

## Alpha
This is an early alpha. Recipes for filling buckets with Monster are coming in a later version.

## Requirements
- Minecraft 26.3
- Fabric Loader 0.20.1+
- Fabric API
